## Part 3: Resume & LinkedIn Polish Checklist

- [ ] Resume is 1 page (2 max for 10+ yrs experience)
- [ ] Every bullet starts with a strong action verb
- [ ] Every bullet has quantified impact (%, $, latency, scale)
- [ ] Most recent role has 4–6 bullets, older roles have 2–3
- [ ] Skills section lists languages/frameworks relevant to target roles
- [ ] No typos (have someone else proofread)
- [ ] LinkedIn headline matches target role (e.g., "Senior Software Engineer | Distributed Systems")
- [ ] LinkedIn summary is 3–4 sentences: who you are, what you do, what you're looking for
- [ ] LinkedIn experience mirrors resume bullets (can be slightly longer)
- [ ] Profile photo is professional and recent
